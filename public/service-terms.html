Assisted setup, then your free month

We agree your venue’s requirements, prepare its installation and check it with you before inviting you to subscribe. Your venue receives its own database, staff accounts and HTTPS address. The service includes hosting, software maintenance, backups and email support for the features described in the user manual. Guest card payments, SMS, POS integrations, a diner marketplace and a shared multi-venue dashboard are not included. Your first calendar month is free, beginning when you complete subscription checkout after your venue is ready.

Price and renewal

Choose £25 per venue per month or £250 per venue per year. Payment is collected upfront after your free month, then renews automatically for the same period until you cancel. Both plans include the same features. There is no setup fee or per-cover commission. Classicbikemerch Ltd is not VAT-registered and does not add VAT. Stripe processes subscription payments; Coverstead does not store full card numbers. Checkout shows the amount and first payment date before you subscribe.

Cancellation and billing problems

You can cancel renewal at any time through Manage billing. Service continues to the end of the free or paid period shown in your billing account. Cancel before the free month ends to avoid your first payment. For an incorrect charge, refund request or immediate cancellation, email us. We assess refund requests individually and honour any rights required by law. If we permanently stop providing the service during a prepaid period, we will refund the unused portion. We will give at least 30 days’ notice of a price or material service change before it applies to a renewal, so you can choose whether to continue.

Failed payments and ending the service

If a payment fails, there is a seven-day grace period to update your payment details. New bookings may then be paused. We preserve staff access to existing bookings and exports for 30 days after service ends, unless access must be restricted to protect security or comply with law. Export your records during that period. We then delete the venue’s operational data; backup copies expire under the retention schedule in our data-processing terms. Tell us promptly if you need an earlier deletion or assistance with an export.

Support and continuity

Email hello@coverstead.net. We aim to reply within two working days, Monday–Friday, excluding English bank holidays. This is an initial-response aim, not a guaranteed resolution time or 24-hour service. We investigate reported defects and maintain the application with reasonable care and skill. We do not promise uninterrupted availability or a particular increase in bookings. Keep a current printable service sheet and a telephone booking fallback. Planned maintenance will be communicated where practicable; urgent security work may need immediate action.

Your responsibilities and your data

You control your booking policies, opening hours, seating rules, staff permissions, guest notices and marketing permissions. Keep passwords private, remove access when staff leave, and notify us promptly of suspected unauthorised access. Only enter information needed to run your venue; avoid unnecessary sensitive details in notes. You retain control of your venue records. Our data-processing terms form part of this agreement, and our privacy notice explains our own account and support processing.
