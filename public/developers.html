A small, inspectable application

Node.js, SQLite snapshots and a plain HTML/JavaScript interface. One process and one database per installation. The repository includes tests, backup tools and a clean-container rehearsal.

Know the boundaries

No shared multi-site dashboard, SMS, POS connection or online guest card payments. Assisted hosted Enterprise is now open for our first three venues: one free month, then £25 per venue per month or £250 per year. We prepare and check each venue before inviting it to subscribe.

We have no reseller or commission programme at present. Contact us to discuss a pilot; no partnership is implied.
