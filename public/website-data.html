Website measurement

Page counts.
No visitor profiles.

We use simple totals to understand which public resources people open.

What these counts contain

When enabled, the app stores the UTC date, a fixed resource label (such as demo, manual or source download) and a count of successful requests. It retains at most 90 UTC calendar dates. It does not store IP addresses, visitor IDs, cookies, query strings, referrers or browser details in these counts. Known crawler and monitoring requests are filtered before counting; their browser information is not saved.

Repeated requests can count more than once. These totals do not tell us how many individual people visited, whether someone installed the app, or whether a demo led to a paying customer.

Other parts of the website

The private enquiry form has its own notice explaining how we handle your contact details and question.

The demo keeps its fictional bookings in your tab’s session storage. Resetting the demo replaces them. Its guided progress stays in the tab too; it is not sent as an analytics event. The venue-fit check runs in your browser. If you choose to enquire, you can review any carried answers before submitting. Staff and test-billing accounts use separate authentication and storage; these activity totals do not include their data. The web host may keep separate technical access and security logs.
