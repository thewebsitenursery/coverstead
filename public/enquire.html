How we use your enquiry

Classicbikemerch Ltd operates Coverstead. We use the details and optional venue context you choose to submit to consider and respond to your enquiry, with your permission. A source or demo answer describes what you tell us; it is not cross-site tracking. We do not use this form to add you to a marketing list, sell your details or make automated decisions about you.

Your enquiry and optional venue answers are saved in our private inbox so Alistair can respond. An email alert contains only a reference, your reply address and a sign-in link. Your question and venue answers are not included in that alert and are not sent to a mailing list or external customer-management service.

For the hosted service, DigitalOcean hosts the inbox in London. Scaleway sends the alert from Paris without adding engagement tracking. Backblaze B2 in Amsterdam stores client-encrypted recovery copies; enquiries have a separate seven-day recovery window and are excluded from general venue archives. Only people handling Coverstead enquiries and operating the service have access.

Live form records expire 90 days after submission. We remove enquiry emails from the active company mailbox within 90 days after the conversation ends, unless a service, dispute or legal obligation requires a record. Failed recovery/pruning jobs can extend backup windows and are investigated. Before recovery, expiry/deletion instructions are reapplied and old email alerts are suppressed. The privacy notice separately explains provider email logs, delivery metadata, transfers and legacy Opalstack copies; the seven-day window is not a promise about every provider-held copy.

Replies and support correspondence use hello@coverstead.net, our existing Opalstack mailbox in Germany. Its contractual and transfer arrangements remain under review, as described in the privacy notice. Its infrastructure backups can retain deleted correspondence for up to 12 weeks; individual messages cannot be removed from existing snapshots. No replacement mailbox is being introduced for this launch.

You can withdraw permission, ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your details, or raise a concern through this form or by emailing hello@coverstead.net. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful handling. You may also raise a concern with the ICO. Supplying an email and question is necessary for a reply; you can explore the demo without submitting any personal information.

Alistair handles enquiries for Classicbikemerch Ltd. Please use a business contact address where possible. We normally aim to reply within two working days.

This notice covers enquiries only. Venue booking data and managed-service accounts have separate purposes and requirements.
