How we use your enquiry

Classicbikemerch Ltd operates Coverstead. We use the details you choose to submit to consider and respond to your enquiry, with your permission. We do not use this form to add you to a marketing list, sell your details or make automated decisions about you.

Enquiries are held in a private inbox on the Coverstead server, accessible to the operator through protected administration tools. Live enquiry records are deleted 90 days after submission. We have not connected this inbox to email delivery or an external customer-management service.

You can withdraw permission, ask for access, correction or deletion, or raise a concern through this form or by emailing the contact shown when enquiries open. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful handling. You may also raise a concern with the ICO. Supplying an email and question is necessary for a reply; you can explore the demo without submitting any personal information.

This notice covers enquiries only. Venue booking data and future managed-service accounts have separate purposes and requirements.
