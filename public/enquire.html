How we use your enquiry

Classicbikemerch Ltd operates Coverstead. We use the details and optional venue context you choose to submit to consider and respond to your enquiry, with your permission. A source or demo answer describes what you tell us; it is not cross-site tracking. We do not use this form to add you to a marketing list, sell your details or make automated decisions about you.

Your enquiry is saved in our private server inbox and emailed to our company mailbox so Alistair can respond. The email includes the details you submit; it is not sent to a mailing list or external customer-management service. Live form records are deleted automatically 90 days after submission. We retain enquiry emails while the conversation is active and remove them within 90 days after it ends. If you later become a customer, any records needed for that service will be covered separately.

Our hosting and email provider is Opalstack LLC, a US company whose privacy policy describes its international processing and transfer safeguards. Access is restricted to those handling Coverstead enquiries and operating the service. We do not include the form inbox in our long-term application archives. The host maintains rotating recovery backups: three daily, two weekly and two monthly copies. Deleted records may remain in those backups until they rotate out; they are not used for routine enquiries. Before any recovery, we reapply expiry and deletion requests.

You can withdraw permission, ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your details, or raise a concern through this form or by emailing the contact shown when enquiries open. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful handling. You may also raise a concern with the ICO. Supplying an email and question is necessary for a reply; you can explore the demo without submitting any personal information.

Alistair handles enquiries for Classicbikemerch Ltd. Please use a business contact address where possible. We normally aim to reply within two working days.

This notice covers enquiries only. Venue booking data and future managed-service accounts have separate purposes and requirements.
