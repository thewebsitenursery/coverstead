For independent pubs serving food

A full dining room.
At your kitchen’s pace.

A free table does not always mean the kitchen is ready for another six orders. Coverstead combines a booking diary with arrival limits, table rules and space for the walk-ins who make a pub a pub.

Free open-source Community edition. One venue per installation. Early pilot.

Built from a village pub

Coverstead grew from the reservation app at The Red Lion in Kilsby. Alistair wanted to share a useful tool with other independent venues. You can inspect the source, adapt it and run it on your own server.

It is a way to manage the guests who find you through your website, phone or front door. It does not provide a marketplace of diners or promise to bring extra bookings by itself.

Work through a typical Sunday lunch

Before the doors open

Set your actual tables, permitted combinations, dining duration and kitchen arrival limit. Check a two-person booking, a larger table and a full time slot. The kitchen limit controls guests arriving within a rolling window, not just the number of empty seats.

During service

Keep online bookings, phone reservations and walk-ins in one diary. Change the guest status as people arrive. Print the service sheet for the team, with staff comments kept away from the customer booking page.

When plans change

Amend a reservation or block a dining session with the existing bookings visible. Agree large-party arrangements with the guest. Deposits taken elsewhere can be recorded manually; the current app does not collect cards or charge no-show fees.

After the visit

Keep a customer visit history and record marketing permission separately. The loyalty tools let staff issue a single-use reward when a customer qualifies. Email delivery requires a configured and tested mail service.

Is it a fit for your pub?

Start here if you run one venue, want control of your bookings and can arrange a developer or host for a pilot installation. Community has no software licence fee or per-cover commission; hosting and email still have costs.

If your first requirement is automatic card guarantees, guest self-service changes, SMS, a POS connection or a central dashboard across several venues, those are gaps in this release. Check the manual before you switch.
