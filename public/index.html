From one independent to another

Born in a village pub.
Built to give something back.

Running a busy, independent village pub is a labour of love. For years, we kept our reservations in a paper diary. Spending more than £100 a month on booking software felt like another cost we simply couldn’t justify.

With the help of modern AI tools, I began building a reservation app around the way our pub actually works. I could shape it to suit our tables, our kitchen and our team. That was the beginning of Coverstead.

Coverstead is built for small, independent pubs, cafés and restaurants. It brings bookings, table planning and customer information together, helping venues welcome more guests, run smoother services and stay in touch with customers who have agreed to hear from them.

It helped my team, brought in more bookings and improved the service we could give our customers. I want other independent venues to have that opportunity too.

That’s why Coverstead’s self hosted version is free and open source. It’s my way of giving something useful back to an industry I love and am part of, at a time when many of us could use a little breathing room.

Alistair · The Red Lion, Kilsby

A few things you might wonder

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is it really built for a small pub or restaurant?

Yes. The starting point was a working village pub: real table combinations, busy kitchens, phone bookings, walk-ins and regulars. You can start with the everyday essentials and add detail at your own pace.

Do we pay for every booking?

No. Coverstead charges no per-cover commission. Enterprise includes your first month free, then costs £10 per venue per month or £100 per year paid upfront, with the same features in both. Community has no software licence fee; you arrange your own hosting and email service.

Can we keep using our existing website?

Yes. Add a link or a “Book a table” button to your website and social profiles. Guests reach your venue’s branded booking page. No website rebuild is required.

What happens to our customer data?

With self hosting, it stays in your own database. You can export bookings and permission-based marketing contacts. Email marketing permission is kept separate from reservation messages.

Does it take card payments or send texts?

This release records deposits taken by phone or in person. Email delivery needs your SMTP setup. SMS, automated refunds and a general online checkout are future integrations, not included promises.

Is the first month really free?

Yes. Your first month on Enterprise is free, with all Enterprise features included. It gives you and your team time to set up your venue, use it through real services and see how it fits. After that, choose £10 per venue per month or £100 for a year paid upfront. Managed service enrolment is not open yet; this preview does not start a trial or take payment.

Can we pay annually instead of monthly?

Yes. After your free first month, pay £100 upfront for a year per venue. The annual plan includes exactly the same features as monthly. Community remains free to install yourself.
