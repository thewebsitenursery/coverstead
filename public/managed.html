One simple price

First month free. Then £25 per venue per month, or £250 for a year paid upfront. The same Enterprise features in both options. No setup fee or per-cover commission.

Coverstead is operated by Classicbikemerch Ltd. The company is not VAT-registered; no VAT is added at present.

What you can try today

Bookings, table rules, kitchen pacing, walk-ins, customer records, loyalty and staff tools are in the source. The public demo shows the core booking and diary workflow.

What hosting includes

Hosting, updates, encrypted backups and email support for your separate venue installation. Read the service terms and privacy notice before you decide.

How you get started

Tell us about your venue, opening hours and table setup. We’ll agree what you need, prepare your staff access and booking page, then check bookings and email with you. Your first calendar month begins only when your venue is ready and you complete Stripe checkout. You can cancel before that month ends to avoid your first payment.

SMS, online guest card payments, POS integrations and Reserve with Google are not included in the current app.

Help us build the right thing

Put it through a sample service.

Make a sample booking, find it at the staff desk, and decide what your team would need before using it. You can ask us privately about your venue or share feedback publicly on GitHub. Do not include guest details or private contact information.
