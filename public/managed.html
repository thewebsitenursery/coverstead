The intended price

First month free. Then £10 per venue per month, or £100 for a year paid upfront. The same Enterprise features in both options. No setup fee or per-cover commission.

Coverstead is operated by Classicbikemerch Ltd. The company is not VAT-registered; no VAT is added at present.

What you can try today

Bookings, table rules, kitchen pacing, walk-ins, customer records, loyalty and staff tools are in the source. The public demo shows the core booking and diary workflow.

What hosting will cover

The planned service covers hosting, updates, backups and support for a separate venue installation. Support arrangements and service terms will be published before enrolment opens.

What still needs to be ready

Reliable venue provisioning, customer email operations, live billing readiness, service terms and ongoing operational monitoring. Email verification and password recovery are currently restricted to controlled test accounts. Stripe is currently test-only.

SMS, online guest card payments, POS integrations and Reserve with Google are not included in the current app.
