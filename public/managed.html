Before you decide

Do I need a new website or a technical person?

No new website is needed. We prepare your hosted booking page and help you add a “Book a table” link to your existing site. Your website administrator may need to add the link. You can also share that address directly with guests.

What happens to the bookings already in my diary?

Keep your current diary in use while we prepare and practise. Before switching, check and enter future reservations with the staff tools, then agree which diary the team will use. The current customer CSV import does not import reservations. The switching checklist explains the handover.

When does the free month start, and how do I cancel?

Your first calendar month starts when your venue is ready and you complete Stripe checkout. Checkout shows the first payment date. Cancel renewal through Manage billing before that date to avoid the first payment. An enquiry or demo does not start the clock.

Who helps if we get stuck?

Email hello@coverstead.net. We aim to reply within two working days; this is not a 24-hour support service. We’ll show your team the manual and printable service sheet during setup. See the service terms for the full support and cancellation arrangements.
