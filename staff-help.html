Using the diary & printing

The main buttons are Add a booking, Print service sheet, Block a dining session, Customers & loyalty and Refresh. The Help button in the header opens this guide.

  1. Choose a date and select Daily view, Weekly view, Monthly view or All bookings / customer search.
  2. Filter by lunch or dinner, and by regular bookings or Christmas Parties. Search by the customer’s name, email, telephone or booking reference. Clear the search and filters to see everything again.
  3. Weekly and monthly views have day tiles. Select one to open its daily diary. Each booking shows its date, service, contact details, time, party size, tables, requirements and status.
  4. Use Refresh to fetch the latest changes from other staff or online bookings. The view does not continuously update by itself.
  5. Choose Print service sheet, select a date and Lunch, Dinner or both, then open the printable sheet. This includes all non-cancelled bookings for the selected service, regardless of diary search filters. Every ten-minute slot is pre-filled, with empty rows for handwriting and a blank Status column. Lunch and dinner print separately. Use A4 portrait at 100% scale and switch off browser headers and footers. You can also save it as a PDF. Very busy services or long requirements may continue onto another page.

The summary totals follow the current filters and exclude cancelled bookings. Dates in diary text use DD/MM/YYYY; times are local to Kilsby. Session closures appear as notices in the relevant date and service view.

Adding a staff booking

  1. Choose Add a booking. If the customer is already on file, select them under Look up an existing customer to fill in their contact details.
  2. Choose the booking type, date, number of guests and arrival time.
  3. Enter the customer’s full name and telephone number. Email is optional for staff bookings.
  4. For up to 12 guests, leave Assign automatically selected or choose a suitable table or standard combination. For larger groups, see the next section.
  5. Record dietary needs, allergies and special requirements. Use staff notes for arrangements the team needs to know about.
  6. Tick deposit received only if payment has actually been taken. For a short-notice group requiring a pre-order, tick the kitchen agreement only after arranging it with the kitchen.
  7. Only tick the email offers box if the customer has explicitly agreed. Then choose Save staff booking.

The form stays within the staff area. A saved booking appears in the diary. If an email address was given, the customer receives a booking email. Staff-entered bookings do not send a notification to the pub. With no email address, give the customer their details and reference over the phone.

Adding walk-ins

Use the teal Add Walk-in button at the far right of the diary buttons for guests who are eating now without a reservation.

  1. Enter the number of people sitting.
  2. Tick their assigned table or tables. The selected tables must have enough seats. Unavailable tables are shown but cannot be selected.
  3. Choose Seat walk-in · 90 minutes. The booking starts at the current local time, is marked Arrived and holds those tables for 90 minutes.

No name, phone, email or deposit is required, and no emails are sent. Walk-ins do not create anonymous customer profiles or loyalty visits. Walk-ins can be seated even when the kitchen’s normal 16-cover arrival limit is exceeded. A warning appears after saving: tell the customer they can be seated, but there may be a slight wait for food. The warning also stays on the diary and is recorded in the walk-in’s notes. Walk-ins still count towards arrival totals for other bookings.

The system checks future reservations as well as guests currently seated: a table reserved during the next 90 minutes cannot be assigned. Walk-ins cannot be added outside an open service or during a blocked session. Use Manage to update attendance or cancel a mistaken entry. Mark a booking Completed once its guests have left and the table is ready: this immediately releases the table for reuse, subject to any upcoming reservation. The table becomes available for bookings after the 90-minute hold ends, even if the row still says Arrived.

Large parties & rearranged tables

Customers can book a maximum of 12 online. Staff can enter larger parties, including 30 or more, without a fixed party-size limit.

  1. Enter the full guest count. For more than 12 guests, a table selection area appears.
  2. Select every table you will use or move. You can choose any combination for this large party. Staff must check that the actual layout seats everyone; the app does not enforce the usual seat totals for this arrangement.
  3. Agree the seating and serving arrangements with the kitchen. Tick I have arranged seating for everyone and agreed how the kitchen will serve this party.
  4. Save the booking. This explicit agreement overrides the normal 16-cover arrival limit for that party. It does not allow a table to overlap another booking or bypass a blocked session.

The full guest count still affects availability for other ordinary bookings around that arrival time. Use staff notes to record any staggered meal service or layout plan.

To change the tables later, open Manage. For a rearranged large party, hold Ctrl on Windows or Command on a Mac while selecting the required tables, then save. The large-party arrangement is highlighted in the booking details.

Managing bookings & attendance

Choose Manage beside a booking to see its contact details, requirements and reference. You can change the arrival time, number of guests and assigned tables, update the status, record deposit or pre-order receipt, and add staff notes. Choose Save booking changes when finished.

StatusWhen to use it
Awaiting Christmas depositA Christmas reservation is holding its space but is not confirmed until its deposit is recorded.
ConfirmedThe booking is expected to attend.
ArrivedThe customer has arrived.
CompletedThe customer actually visited. Mark it after the booking time; this is the status that counts towards loyalty.
No-showThe customer did not attend. It does not count as a completed visit.
CancelledThe booking is removed from the active diary and its tables and arrival capacity are released.

Status-only changes do not check kitchen capacity: you can mark guests Arrived, Completed or No-show even during a busy service. Completed releases the table immediately. Moving a booking or changing the guest count still checks kitchen capacity under the usual rules. Restoring a cancelled booking still checks table clashes and service closures.

A booking is not automatically marked Completed. Unpaid Christmas bookings cannot be marked Confirmed, Arrived or Completed.

To change the guest count, enter the new number in Manage and choose suitable tables, then save. Table clashes and the kitchen limit are checked. Parties over 12 require the seating and kitchen agreement unless already arranged as a large party. Deposit requirements update automatically; if the amount increases, record payment only once the full amount is covered, collecting only the difference where money is already held. Christmas Parties return to awaiting deposit until this is recorded. Changing the guest count clears the pre-order received tick so staff can check the revised order. Updated details are emailed using the booking’s normal notification rules. Staff comments are visible in the diary and printed service sheet, but are not added to the customer email.

To change the arrival time, choose a new time in Manage and save. Times are on the same date and can move between lunch and dinner when both services are open. The app checks table clashes, service closures and the kitchen limit before saving. The reference, deposit and pre-order records stay with the booking, and updated details are emailed using the usual notification rules. If the selected tables are unavailable, choose another suitable table or time.

The Manage form does not currently edit the booking’s date or original contact details. For a reservation change that needs those fields, note the reference, cancel the old reservation and create the replacement. Carry over agreed arrangements and accurately record any deposit already held; avoid asking the customer to pay twice. Updating a customer profile does not rewrite an existing reservation.

Cancelling & restoring a booking

  1. Open Manage, then choose Cancel booking & remove from diary.
  2. Read and confirm the prompt. The booking disappears from the active diary and its tables and kitchen capacity become available again.
  3. The record stays in customer history. To find it, tick Show cancelled and check your date, service and search filters.

Cancellation emails follow the original booking’s notification rules. Cancelling does not automatically refund a deposit; arrange any refund separately.

To restore a cancelled booking, show cancelled records, open Manage, choose the appropriate status and save. The app checks table availability, arrival capacity and session closures again. If it refuses, resolve the conflict or arrange a different booking. Christmas deposits must still be recorded before confirmation.

Christmas Party bookings

Select Christmas Party as the booking type. The configured 2026 season is 25/11/2026–23/12/2026, Wednesday to Saturday, within normal service times. Sundays show as sold out for Christmas Parties and cannot accept new Christmas reservations. Regular Sunday lunch is unchanged. Christmas Parties are also unavailable on Mondays and Tuesdays.

A new party is Awaiting deposit unless staff record payment when entering it. Its tables and arrival capacity are held while waiting; it does not expire automatically. Recording the £5-per-person deposit confirms it and sends the applicable customer confirmation. If it will not go ahead, cancel it to release the space.

Christmas rows have a Christmas Party label and a reminder to decorate the table. Use the Christmas filter to find them. Every Christmas Party needs its special menu pre-order, regardless of party size.

Blocking a dining session

Use this for a private event, wake, party or kitchen closure affecting the whole dining area.

  1. Choose Block a dining session.
  2. Choose the date, then Lunch, Dinner or All day. Enter a clear event name or reason.
  3. Choose Block session. If there are existing reservations, the app warns you and asks whether to continue.

The block prevents new online and manual bookings for the selected service. Existing bookings stay in the diary and are not automatically cancelled or contacted. Review them and contact customers separately. The closure notice shows how many existing bookings remain.

A block covers the whole session, not just a start time. For example, blocking dinner for an evening party also prevents earlier dinner arrivals. All day covers the services normally offered on that date. To remove a block, find its notice in the diary and choose Reopen session, then confirm.

Customers & contact details

  1. Choose Customers & loyalty. Search by name, email or telephone.
  2. Filter for all customers, customers ready for a loyalty gift, customers with email permission, or Christmas Party contacts.
  3. Choose Open profile to see contact details, completed visit counts, the last visit, booking history and vouchers.
  4. Edit the name, telephone, customer notes or email permission and save. Use Back to reservations to return to the diary.

Booking records normally link customers by email. For records without email, matching name and telephone can identify the same person. Look up an existing customer when entering a booking to help avoid duplicates. Different people sharing a phone number are not automatically treated as one person.

The profile editor currently changes name, telephone and notes, but not the email address. Ask the manager for help with an incorrect email or duplicate profiles. Customer notes are for the team; requirements specific to a visit should also be recorded on that booking.

Adding paper records & importing old customers

A single customer or past visit

In Customers & loyalty, choose Add customer / paper record. Supply a name and at least an email or telephone. Add a past visit date and time if a completed visit is known; leave the date blank for a contact-only record. Choose Regular table or Christmas Party as appropriate. A Christmas contact with no visit date is tagged without adding an invented visit.

To add another historical visit for an existing customer, open their profile and use Add a paper visit. Enter its date, time, booking type and old reference if known. Only actual past completed visits should be added. Repeated references, or the same date and time without a reference, are skipped for that customer.

A CSV file from another system

  1. Choose Import CSV and download the template. Keep its column names.
  2. Use one row per customer or completed visit. Repeat contact details on each row when importing several visits. Leave visit_date blank for contacts only.
  3. Use DD/MM/YYYY for dates and HH:MM for time. Existing year-first dates are also accepted. Booking type is standard or christmas. A missing visit time defaults to 12:00.
  4. Select the CSV file or paste its contents, then choose Preview import. Review the matches, new customers, skipped duplicates and any errors. Preview does not import anything.
  5. Correct every error and preview again. Choose Confirm import when the preview is right.

Use a file under 500 KB with no more than 1,000 data rows. A preview expires after 15 minutes. Imports preserve existing customer details and fill missing information rather than overwrite them. If you include a status column, only completed, visited or attended records can be imported as visits. Importing history does not reserve tables or send booking confirmations.

Loyalty gifts & vouchers

A customer is highlighted after 10 completed, previously unrewarded visits within a rolling 12 months. Gifts are a staff decision: the app does not automatically issue or send them. One booking counts as one visit for its lead customer, regardless of party size. Future, cancelled and no-show bookings do not qualify.

  1. Open Customers & loyalty and filter Ready for a loyalty gift, or follow the diary’s eligibility prompt.
  2. Open the customer’s profile. In the voucher section, choose a money-off gift or percentage discount, its value, and any offer terms.
  3. Choose printing / handing over, or email. Email delivery requires an email address and recorded email permission.
  4. Choose Issue gift voucher and confirm. The voucher uses ten qualifying visits, has a unique code and expires six calendar months after issue.
  5. Use Open / print voucher to print it or save a copy. When the customer uses it, find the voucher in their profile, choose Mark redeemed and confirm.

Vouchers are single use. Expired or redeemed vouchers cannot be redeemed again in the app. Recording redemption does not apply a discount to the till: staff must handle the bill using the pub’s usual process. Further qualifying visits can earn another staff-approved gift.

Email permission & customer exports

Booking contact details alone do not mean a customer agreed to marketing. Only record permission when the customer explicitly agrees. In a profile, enter a note of when and how they agreed before granting email permission. CSV imports require both marketing_email=yes and a consent_note to record permission.

Export opted-in email contacts downloads a CSV of customers with an email address and recorded permission. The export includes contact details, completed visit count, last visit and consent date. The app does not have a bulk promotional email sender.

Customers can unsubscribe using the link in loyalty emails. Staff can also untick permission in the profile and save. This removes pending loyalty emails; necessary booking correspondence can still be sent.

Arrival slots are 10 minutes apart. Sunday lunch service ends at 16:00; dinner service ends at 21:00. Tables are currently reserved for 90 minutes. New bookings can be made up to a year ahead, subject to service and seasonal restrictions.

The standard kitchen limit is 16 arriving guests in any rolling 30 minutes. It is not a fixed half-hour clock slot: nearby arrival times are counted together. Staff large-party agreements and walk-ins are exceptions. Walk-ins show a busy-kitchen warning rather than being refused. Tables already allocated to an overlapping booking cannot be booked again.

Standard table combinations are 1 + 3 for up to 12, 9 + 10 for up to 8, and 11 + 12 + 13 for up to 12. Automatic assignment picks the smallest suitable available table or permitted combination. Staff-arranged parties over 12 can use other combinations.

Emails & signing in

Online bookings email both the customer and the pub. Staff bookings email only the customer if an email was provided. Messages come from booking@reservations.redlionkilsby.co.uk; replies go to info@redlionkilsby.co.uk. Booking emails include the relevant menus and pre-order form. Christmas emails use the Christmas documents.

Emails are queued after saving. A booking can be saved even if email delivery is delayed. If the diary shows waiting emails, use Refresh after a short while. If the warning persists, ask the manager to investigate rather than create another booking.

Use the staff password supplied by the manager. Sign out when finished, especially on a shared device. A sign-in lasts up to eight hours and may end earlier after an app update or restart. The customer booking page link in the header opens the public form; use Add a booking for staff entry.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a time unavailable when there are empty tables?

The kitchen’s rolling 30-minute arrival limit may already be reached, the suitable tables may overlap another booking, or the service may be blocked. Check nearby arrival times, the required table size and the closure notices. A pending Christmas deposit also holds space.

Can I save a phone booking without an email address?

Yes. Staff bookings require the full name and telephone number; email is optional. If it is blank, no booking email is sent for that staff reservation. Confirm the details verbally.

Can we book a party of 30, 40 or more?

Yes, through Add a booking. Select the tables being rearranged and confirm the seating and kitchen agreement. Check the physical layout and serving plan first. The customer form remains limited to 12.

Does ticking “deposit received” charge the customer?

No. Take payment through the pub’s usual process first, then tick the box and save. Online deposit payment is currently off.

Why is a Christmas Party still awaiting its deposit?

Until staff record payment, it is a reservation holding space rather than a confirmed party. Open Manage, record the paid deposit and save. If the party will not proceed, cancel it; unpaid reservations do not expire automatically.

Does a regular large group also pay £5 per person?

No. A regular booking of 10 or more needs £25 for the booking. The £5-per-person deposit applies to Christmas Parties and replaces that £25 deposit.

Can I enter a group booking less than 48 hours ahead?

Staff can do so after agreeing the pre-order with the kitchen and ticking the short-notice agreement in Add a booking. Do not tick it before those arrangements are agreed.

Will blocking dinner cancel bookings already in the diary?

No. Existing bookings remain, and staff see a warning. Contact those customers and cancel or rearrange their reservations separately. The block prevents new reservations for the whole selected session.

How do I change a booking’s date, time or guest count?

You can change the guest count in Manage and choose suitable tables. You can also change the arrival time in Manage without cancelling. To change the date, record the old details, cancel the old reservation and create a replacement. Carry forward the correct deposit status and arrangements, and explain the replacement to the customer.

Where did a cancelled booking go?

Tick Show cancelled. Check the date range and other filters, or choose All bookings and search the name or reference. Cancellation preserves the record while releasing the tables.

Why has a customer’s visit count not increased?

Check that the reservation is marked Completed after the visit. Confirmed and Arrived do not count yet. Also check that the visit is on the correct customer profile. Paper visits can be added to that profile if they are genuine completed visits.

Why does someone with ten visits not qualify for a gift?

The ten visits must be completed, within the last rolling 12 months, and not already used for a previous voucher. The profile shows total visits separately from qualifying visits.

Why can’t I email a loyalty voucher?

The customer needs both an email address and explicit email permission. Otherwise choose a printed voucher to hand over. Booking email permission and marketing permission are separate.

Will a CSV import send emails or create future reservations?

No. It adds customer contacts and completed visit history. Use Add a booking for upcoming reservations. Preview and correct the import before confirming it.

A customer has not received their booking email. What should I do?

Confirm the email address they supplied, ask them to check spam or junk, and check for an email warning on the diary. A saved diary booking still exists if an email is delayed. Ask the manager to investigate persistent problems; do not create a duplicate reservation just to resend an email.

Why am I being asked to sign in again?

The session may have expired, or the app may have restarted during an update. Sign in again and check whether your last change was saved before entering it a second time.

I cannot see a new button or a recent change. What should I try?

Reload the browser page. If necessary, use Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows or Command + Shift + R on a Mac. The Refresh button inside the diary refreshes its data; a browser reload also loads updated page controls.

Getting support

For access, payment questions, incorrect customer records or a persistent error, contact the manager. Give the booking reference, date and time, the action you were taking and the exact message shown. A screenshot can help. Avoid sending passwords or card details.

Before repeating a failed action, check the diary or customer profile to see whether it saved. For a payment or kitchen arrangement, confirm with the team directly.
