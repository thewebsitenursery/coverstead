Privacy notice

Prepared for the DigitalOcean launch — not yet in force. The current website and mailbox remain on their existing services. Hosted enrolment is closed. The company-mailbox section below awaits provider clarification. Read the current public privacy notice until the move is complete.

Who is responsible

CLASSICBIKEMERCH LTD (company 17321186), 66 Paul Street, London, England, United Kingdom, EC2A 4NA, operates Coverstead. Contact hello@coverstead.net. We are the controller for enquiries, subscription accounts, billing administration and support records. Each venue is the controller for its guests’ and staff’s information; we host that information on the venue’s instructions under our data-processing terms.

Information and purposes

We use account-holder names, email addresses, authentication records, subscription identifiers, service history and support correspondence to set up, secure and support Coverstead. We process customer account information to perform our contract; business-contact and security information for our legitimate interests in operating a reliable service; and accounting information to meet legal obligations. Enquiries use the permission described on the form. We do not use enquiry permission for marketing.

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Stripe collects payment details; we receive subscription identifiers and payment status, not full card numbers. We obtain account information from you or your organisation and payment-status information from Stripe. Venue records come from the venue, its staff and guests under the venue’s own notice. If you do not provide the contact or billing information needed for an account, we cannot provide that account; you can explore the public demo without submitting it.

Enquiries and website activity

The enquiry form explains its separate consent and 90-day retention arrangements. Enquiring does not subscribe you to marketing. Optional website activity counts contain daily resource totals, not visitor profiles or analytics cookies. The demo keeps fictional data in your browser tab. Staff and billing sign-in use essential session cookies; verification and recovery links expire and can be used once. The host also processes technical access and security logs to operate and protect the service.

Providers and locations

For the migrated service, DigitalOcean hosts the Coverstead application in London, United Kingdom. Backblaze B2 stores our encrypted off-server backups in EU Central, Amsterdam, Netherlands. Encryption protects the backup contents; the application does not receive the storage credentials or backup password. Each provider may also process service-account, billing, support and security information under its own arrangements.

Scaleway Transactional Email in Paris, France sends booking, account-verification, password-recovery, enquiry and operational notifications. Booking emails omit private requirements and staff notes. Enquiry alerts contain a reference, reply address and private-inbox link; the visitor’s question stays in that inbox. The sending service adds no engagement pixel or rewritten link. The recipient’s own email provider separately handles the delivered message.

Stripe handles subscription payments and payment authentication under its privacy policy. We do not send it venue reservation databases. We do not sell personal information. Our data-processing terms identify the venue-data service providers and their roles.

The selected storage locations do not mean that all provider administration or support stays in the UK. DigitalOcean and Backblaze provide processing agreements with transfer provisions; we use the safeguards applicable to the actual service and destination, including adequacy arrangements or current UK contractual safeguards where required. Contact hello@coverstead.net for details or a copy of applicable safeguards, with necessary commercial/security redactions. The company-mailbox arrangement remains the specific unresolved item below.

Company mailbox — awaiting confirmation

The company contact remains hello@coverstead.net. Incoming mail currently uses Opalstack. Namecheap Private Email is the proposed replacement, but no mailbox has been ordered or moved. Before this notice takes effect, this section will identify the selected mailbox provider, its processing locations, the applicable UK transfer mechanism, support access and expiry of residual backup copies. We will preserve incoming mail and existing correspondence during any separately tested mailbox move.

Retention

Live enquiry-form records expire 90 days after submission. They have a separate encrypted seven-day recovery window and are excluded from general venue backups. We remove enquiry correspondence from the active company mailbox within 90 days after a conversation ends, unless records are needed for an agreed service, a dispute or a legal obligation. Mailbox-provider backup expiry is separate and will be identified in the final mailbox section.

Service-account and support records are normally retained during the service and for up to 12 months after closure. Accounting records are retained for six years from the end of the financial year they relate to, or longer where legally required. Necessary records may be retained for unresolved disputes or legal obligations. Venue data follows the instructions and closure schedule in the data-processing terms.

Scaleway deletes message content after recipient-server acceptance or a hard bounce, or after 36 hours of failed delivery attempts. Content classified as spam can remain for 72 hours; a blocked-domain hold can last 15 days. It retains encrypted infrastructure backups for seven days. Sending metadata and logs remain while the sending domain is active; specific-record deletion can be requested through support. These periods follow its email privacy policy and do not describe recipients’ mailbox retention.

We review application security and operational logs and normally remove them within 90 days. Provider-held logs follow the provider’s operational retention. Failed backup/pruning schedules can extend recovery windows and require investigation. Deletions and corrections are reapplied before restored records return to use; recovered enquiry notifications are not replayed.

The move does not erase old copies immediately. Existing Opalstack infrastructure backups can retain former Coverstead application or mailbox data for up to 12 weeks; individual records cannot be removed from existing snapshots. We will retire active Coverstead copies under the recorded migration/closure plan. This does not involve closing the shared hosting account or other applications.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent where we rely on it without affecting earlier lawful processing. We may need to verify your identity. For a booking or a venue staff record, contact the venue first; we assist it with requests. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Changes and contact

We will update this notice when our processing changes and notify affected customers of material changes. Send privacy or security concerns to hello@coverstead.net; please avoid putting guest records in public GitHub issues.
