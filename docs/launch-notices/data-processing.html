Data-processing terms

Prepared for the DigitalOcean launch — not yet in force. The current website and mailbox remain on their existing services. Hosted enrolment is closed. The company-mailbox section below awaits provider clarification. Read the current public privacy notice until the move is complete.

Roles, purpose and duration

The subscribing venue is the controller; CLASSICBIKEMERCH LTD, company 17321186, is its processor for hosting, maintaining and supporting its reservation installation. These terms apply during the service and the closure/backup period. The venue instructs us to store, organise, retrieve, back up, transmit and delete records as needed for the documented service and its authorised requests. We will process venue data only on those instructions, including transfers, unless law requires otherwise; where permitted we will tell the venue of that requirement.

People and information

Records may concern guests, venue staff and administrators: names, contact details, reservations, service notes, visit history, marketing permissions, staff roles and authentication records. Guests may supply dietary or accessibility information. The venue must identify an appropriate lawful basis and any additional condition for sensitive data, provide suitable notices, and minimise free-text information. We do not use venue guest data for our own advertising.

Security and confidentiality

Access is limited to people who need it for the service and are subject to confidentiality obligations. Each hosted venue has a separate application process, database, configuration and staff accounts. We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, role checks, private configuration files, encrypted off-server backups, software maintenance and operational checks. These controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee that incidents will never occur. The venue remains responsible for its users, devices and appropriate staff permissions.

Sub-processors

For the migrated hosted service, the venue gives general written authorisation for DigitalOcean to host its application in London, United Kingdom; Backblaze B2 to store encrypted backups in EU Central, Amsterdam, Netherlands; and Scaleway Transactional Email to send transactional messages in Paris, France. Booking messages exclude private requirements and staff notes. Stripe processes the separate subscription relationship; this integration does not send it guest reservation databases. A recipient’s mailbox provider is selected by the recipient. Any additional provider chosen for a venue is recorded and agreed during setup.

We engage a venue-data sub-processor only under a written arrangement imposing the applicable data-protection obligations, and remain responsible to the venue for its performance of those obligations. Transfers follow documented venue instructions and the applicable legal safeguards; details are available on request. We will notify the venue at least 30 days before adding or replacing a venue-data sub-processor and allow objections on reasonable data-protection grounds. We will discuss an alternative or ending the affected service if an objection cannot be resolved.

The company mailbox used for support is still being settled. Before these terms take effect, its provider, processing locations, relevant transfer safeguards and retention exceptions will be included here and in the privacy notice. Please do not send guest records in an ordinary support email; use a reference and agree a suitable private transfer if access is needed.

Assistance and incidents

We assist the venue, taking account of the processing and information available to us, with individual-rights requests, security obligations, impact assessments and regulatory consultation. Requests received directly from guests are referred to the venue. We notify the venue without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting it, provide known facts and subsequent updates, and help investigate and mitigate the incident. The venue decides its regulatory and individual notifications. Report concerns to hello@coverstead.net.

Backups, return and deletion

The hosted service takes hourly local snapshots, normally kept for 48 hours, and daily encrypted off-server snapshots, normally kept for 35 days. The last successful recovery point is retained if a newer one has not been verified. Failed jobs can delay expiry; we investigate and record exceptions. These are Coverstead’s recovery settings, separate from provider-held copies.

The venue can export booking and customer records through the app and request a fuller return of its records. At the venue’s choice we return or delete its personal data at the end of the service, deleting remaining operational copies unless law requires retention. Our default closure process leaves 30 days for export, followed by operational deletion; earlier deletion can be instructed. Data in our own encrypted backup repository normally expires within a further 36 days, including the configured deleted-object cleanup. The last-successful-snapshot safeguard is reviewed explicitly when closing a venue so it does not retain a closed venue indefinitely.

Scaleway’s separate email-content, metadata and seven-day infrastructure-backup periods are set out in the privacy notice. Specific email records or logs can require a provider deletion request. DigitalOcean’s processing agreement allows limited legal-retention and isolated-backup exceptions after deletion; that does not promise every provider copy follows our 35-day schedule. We record and disclose any applicable exception to a venue’s deletion instruction.

Old Opalstack copies, where they exist from before migration, remain subject to its up-to-12-week infrastructure-backup window, without targeted deletion from existing snapshots. New venues on DigitalOcean will not be deployed to Opalstack. The company-mailbox retirement and backup arrangements will be recorded separately before these terms take effect.

Before restoring data to use, we reapply recorded deletions and corrections and review service access. We do not use backups for advertising or routine access to deleted records. Recovery credentials are held separately from the web application.

Accountability

We make information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance available to the venue and allow reasonable audits or inspections by it or its appointed auditor, with arrangements that protect other customers’ data and service security. We will tell the venue promptly if an instruction appears to infringe applicable data-protection law. On request at the end of the service, we confirm return/deletion, subject to documented legal-retention obligations. The service terms otherwise continue to apply; these data-processing terms take precedence for the handling of venue personal data.
