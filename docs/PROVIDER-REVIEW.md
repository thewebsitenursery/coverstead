## Email tracking decision

Per-recipient `contactPixelTrackingConsent:false` and the account's consent controls do not remove Brevo's pixel. The [API reference](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/send-transac-email.md) describes anonymised engagement events and reserves `removeTrackingPixel` for Enterprise plans. A received synthetic message still contained the pixel. A human support reply on 18 November confirmed that consent controls retain tracking and that the specialist review declined disabling link tracking for this account. No upgrade, trial, new agreement or charge was requested.

The final email notice must match the active transport. Scaleway now serves staging only. Six synthetic messages reached the operator inbox with SPF/DKIM/DMARC passing; inspected content contained no pixel or rewritten link. This does not describe the old production transport or guarantee all future messages' behaviour. Brevo remains only as an inactive rollback configuration; do not silently restore it and retain a no-tracking claim.

## Remaining email and mailbox decisions

The recipient server marked all six Scaleway messages as spam despite putting them in the Inbox. Scores were 11.02–13.51, versus 2.02–3.43 for earlier Brevo tests and -0.80 for the older local-sendmail booking test. Authentication passing does not identify the cause. Authorized requests ask Scaleway about delivery infrastructure and the account quota, and Opalstack for the actual spam-rule breakdown. Do not invent a reputation diagnosis, weaken filtering or claim general inbox reliability from these samples. After a specific correction, repeat only the affected delivery checks.

On 18 November, a controlled pair tested Opalstack's explanation that a recipient matching Reply-To can inflate its score. Both messages used the same sender, recipient, subject, body and sending IP, with unique message IDs. The matching Reply-To message scored 11.78 and was marked spam; the message without Reply-To scored 6.78 and was not. Both arrived with SPF, DKIM and DMARC passing. This supports a five-point test-address penalty; it does not prove delivery to other providers or justify removing the application's normal reply route. The requested synthetic headers and comparison were supplied in the existing Scaleway diagnostic ticket. The later separate-mailbox check is recorded below; account-specific sending-ceiling confirmation remains outstanding. The controlled comparison itself changed no filter or account access setting.

Subsequent implementation review found the same self-reply pattern in ordinary staff copies and monitoring alerts. The correction preserves guest replies to the venue, gives staff copies the customer's reply address saved with the queued message, and omits self-reply headers for legacy staff copies and monitoring. API and real SMTP regression checks cover the guest/staff distinction and legacy fallback. The correction is deployed in staging: received staff copies reply to the guest and monitoring has no Reply-To; both pass SPF/DKIM/DMARC and contain plain text without tracking. They still carry spam flags (7.99 and 9.12), so the issue is not resolved. Both used the provider's IPv6 sender, unlike the earlier controlled pair; the changed sender and content mean those scores are not a direct comparison. The complete synthetic headers were supplied in the existing support case. The final customer confirmation reached the separate owner-controlled Inbox without a spam flag (score 3.52), with SPF/DKIM/DMARC passing, replies to the venue and private requirements excluded. ## Application recovery and deletion

Hourly consistent venue snapshots keep about 48 hours locally. Daily encrypted venue snapshots use a 35-day recovery window, retaining a last successful snapshot if necessary; closures and deletion requests require operator handling. Separate enquiry snapshots use a seven-day window during successful daily pruning. Failed schedules can extend those windows and raise monitoring alerts. Restore enquiries into a new private directory, omit expired/deleted records, review corrections and suppress email replay. See [ENQUIRIES.md](ENQUIRIES.md).

These are our configured controls, not guarantees about all copies held by infrastructure providers. Record provider exceptions accurately, keep deletion instructions through the recovery window, and retain owner-controlled recovery credentials outside the VM.
